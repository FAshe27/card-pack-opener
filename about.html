About PackRipper

PackRipper is a free online card-pack-opening game. Rip open virtual packs, chase the rare pulls, and build your collection — all for fun, and all play money.

What PackRipper is

In PackRipper, players open virtual card packs and collect cards from dozens of themed sets. You can trade cards with other players, send gifts, post and claim listings and bounties in the Trading Post, complete daily quests, spin the prize wheel, and play arcade mini-games between openings. New themed sets arrive regularly, some cards are gloriously silly, and a few are genuinely rare — every card you pull is saved to your collection.

Everything in PackRipper is play money. Packs, cards, tokens, and tickets exist only inside the game and have no real-world value — they cannot be cashed out or sold. PackRipper is free to play, with no ads. If you enjoy the game, you can chip in voluntarily to help cover hosting costs; donating is purely a way to support the game — it buys nothing and unlocks nothing.

How you sign in

PackRipper uses Google Sign-In, provided through Supabase Auth, so players can create a new account in a few taps or link Google to an account they already have. Either way, your collection and progress stay right where they are.

When you sign in with Google, PackRipper accesses only your basic Google profile information — your name, email address, and profile picture — and uses it only to identify you and to set up or link your account. Our Privacy Policy explains exactly what is stored, how it is used, and how to ask for it to be deleted.

Our commitments

PackRipper does not use Google APIs to generate, facilitate, or distribute AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (AI NCII), or any other prohibited content.

PackRipper has no advertising, and player information is never sold.
