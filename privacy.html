✦ PackRipper

Privacy Policy

PackRipper is a free card-pack-opening game operated by Franklin Ashe as a hobby project. This policy explains what information the game stores, why it is used, and the choices you have.

Effective: October 27, 2026 · Last updated: October 27, 2026

Information PackRipper stores

Account and sign-in information. PackRipper accounts use a username and PIN or, as it rolls out, Google sign-in. PINs are stored only as bcrypt hashes, never in plain text. Session tokens are randomly generated, and only a SHA-256 hash of each token is stored on the server.

Game information. To run your account and save your progress, PackRipper stores packs, card collections, trades, gifts, bounties and Trading Post listings (including listing notes), quest progress, notifications, friends, public display names, and donation-funding progress associated with the game.

Donations. If you voluntarily chip in, Stripe processes the payment. PackRipper does not receive or store your payment-card number. PackRipper records the donation amount and status so it can show the public funding-progress bar.

Google user data

What PackRipper accesses: When you choose Google sign-in, Google provides basic profile information: your name, email address, and profile picture. Supabase Auth handles the sign-in process for PackRipper.

How it is used: PackRipper uses Google user data only to sign you in, create or link your game account, and display the name you choose in the game. PackRipper stores the Google account identifier, email address, and provider linked to your game account, together with your chosen display name.

What PackRipper does not do: Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to build an advertising profile. It is not shared except with the service providers needed to operate PackRipper, as described below.

Retention and deletion: The linked Google information is stored while your PackRipper account exists. You may ask for your account and its data to be deleted by emailing packripperapp@gmail.com.

PackRipper’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

When an existing PackRipper account is linked to Google, that account’s PIN login is turned off and Google becomes its sign-in method.

How information is used

PackRipper uses stored information to operate the game, authenticate players, save collections and progress, enable trades and other player features, keep accounts and public areas safe, respond to support requests, and display donation-funding progress. PackRipper has no advertising. The site code does not include analytics or advertising trackers. Everything in the game is play money.

Sharing and service providers

PackRipper does not sell personal information. Information is processed by the providers needed to run the service: Supabase provides authentication and database services, GitHub Pages hosts the website, and Stripe processes voluntary donations. PackRipper may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the service, its operator, or its players.

Your chosen display name and the game content you make public—such as Trading Post listings and notes—can be visible to other players.

Browser storage

PackRipper uses browser localStorage and sessionStorage to keep your session token and game preferences on your device. PackRipper does not use advertising cookies. Signing out removes the stored session token from localStorage and sessionStorage.

Your choices and rights

You may email packripperapp@gmail.com to ask for a copy of your account information, request a correction, or request deletion of your account and its game data. Deleting an account removes the account and associated game data from PackRipper, subject to any information that must be retained for security, payment, or legal reasons.

Children

PackRipper is not directed to children under 13, and PackRipper does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so it can be reviewed and deleted.

Security

PackRipper is served over HTTPS; the .app domain requires secure connections. Credentials and session tokens are hashed as described above. No online service can guarantee perfect security, but access to account information is limited to what is needed to operate and protect the game.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when PackRipper or its practices change. The effective date at the top will be revised when material changes are published. Continued use of PackRipper after an update means the revised policy applies from its stated effective date.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to packripperapp@gmail.com.
